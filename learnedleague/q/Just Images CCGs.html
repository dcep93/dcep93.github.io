Smith's Blurb

Sure, we all know that games like Magic: the Gathering, Pokemon TCG, and Yu-Gi-Oh! form the core of the Customizable/Collectible Card Game genre...but there's a whole world of CCGs out there! Collect cards, build your own deck, battle it out against other players--ever since Magic set the template, a whole boatload of games showed up on the scene, from the 90s boom (and bust) to the Living Card Game push to the more recent games bubbling onto the market in the 2020s. Whether it's identifying games, identifying cards, or figuring out other important things about them, this quiz will see you racking your brain for the weird little card games you rip booster packs for (most of the time). Shuffle up, it's time to play! While there are a few ways to puzzle into some guesses, I'd suggest you approach it as a ModKos R. If you've gotten experience with a wide variety of games, you'll do well.

How to play an LL One-Day

• Answer the 12 questions.

• Designate 5 questions as Money Questions (by ticking the box next to the question).

• For each question answered correctly, you receive 15 points.

• For each correct Money Question, in addition to the 15, you receive the number of points equal to the percentage of participants who answer the question incorrectly (e.g. if 74% of the players get it wrong, you get 74 money points in addition to the 15).

• You must answer the Money Question correctly to receive the money points.

• The player who earns the most total points wins the LL One-Day.
